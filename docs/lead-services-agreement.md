## Plain-English twin for /terms/

**Lead Capture and Reputation Engine.** Two optional month-to-month
services, separate from the free build and separate from hosting. Lead
Capture ($297/month) sends an automatic text back when you miss a call,
puts your website chat on your phone, and drops every call, text, chat and
form into one inbox with booking attached. Reputation Engine ($197/month,
or $97/month alongside Lead Capture) texts a review request after a job
you mark complete and drafts your replies — AI-assisted, checked by a
person — for your approval. Both run on GoHighLevel, which we operate for
you. Texts and call minutes are included up to a monthly allowance stated
in your agreement, and it's one shared allowance if you take both; if
you're heading past it, we tell you and quote first.

**What it doesn't do.** Nobody answers your phone. There's no person and
no AI picking up — a missed call sends the caller an automated text, and
if they reply, you reply. We don't write reviews and we can't remove them.
And we don't promise more calls, leads, reviews or revenue. The only thing
we guarantee is the price — and if we ever change it, you get 30 days'
written notice and can cancel before it takes effect.

**Texting has to be registered first.** US carriers require a business to
be registered before it can text from a regular 10-digit local number
(it's called A2P 10DLC). We file it in your business's name, with your EIN
and address — or on the sole-proprietor path if you don't have an EIN,
which allows less texting. Carrier review takes days to weeks, we can't
speed it up, and texting is limited until it clears. We tell you where it
stands. The registration fees are inside your monthly price.

**The phone number.** The texting number is provisioned through the
platform on the account we run, and it's used only for you. If you leave,
we'll try to move it — across to another agency on the same platform, or
by porting it into an account in your name. Porting is a carrier process
we don't control, and we won't pretend it's guaranteed. So wherever we can,
we forward from a number you already own, and the number printed on your
truck is then never one we provisioned. If that isn't possible on your
setup, we say so before we start.

**Leaving.** Cancel anytime, no fee and no notice period. Billing stops the
day you cancel and any part-month is refunded pro-rata. Within 5 business
days, at no charge, you get a CSV of your records — your contacts, plus
whatever message history, call logs, form and chat submissions, bookings
and drafted review replies the platform will export, and we tell you in
writing what that covers before we run it. Review requests already sent
can't be recalled; anything scheduled and unsent stops. Reviews already
posted stay on whichever profile they were left on; they were always
yours. And leaving never touches the 60-day bug coverage on your website.
