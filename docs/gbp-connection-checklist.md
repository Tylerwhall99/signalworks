# Google Business Profile Connection — Build Deliverable Checklist

> Internal doc. This is the "Google Business Profile connection" item
> every free build ships with, and the checklist half of catalog ledger
> row A5 (the time-per-client half gets measured on the first few real
> builds). The client owns the profile; we guide and connect — we don't
> take ownership of their listing.

## During the build (from intake answers)

- [ ] Ask (Detailed intake already does): does a profile exist, and can
  the client log in? Three paths:
  - **Exists + access** → proceed.
  - **Exists, no access** → walk them through "Claim this business" /
    access-request flow (Google emails the current owner; can take days
    — start early).
  - **Doesn't exist** → client creates it at business.google.com from
    their own Google account (10 minutes, screen-share offered);
    verification (postcard/phone/video) can take days — start early.

## At launch (30 minutes, with the client's login on a call)

- [ ] **Website field** → the new domain.
- [ ] **Phone** → exactly the number on the site (format identical).
- [ ] **Business name** → the real name, no keyword stuffing (a
  suspension risk Google actively enforces).
- [ ] **Primary category** → their trade (e.g., "HVAC contractor"), plus
  2–3 secondary categories that are true.
- [ ] **Service area** → matches the site's service-area copy.
- [ ] **Hours** → match the site. Special hours noted if seasonal.
- [ ] **Services list** → mirror the site's services (names consistent).
- [ ] **Description** → 2–3 plain sentences from the site's own copy.
- [ ] **Photos** → the client's real photos from intake (trucks, crew,
  finished jobs). No stock.
- [ ] **Appointment link** → the site's contact/intake anchor.
- [ ] Confirm NAP consistency (name/address/phone identical across site,
  profile, and footer).

## Hand back

- [ ] Client confirms they can log in on their own phone.
- [ ] One-paragraph note in the owner's guide: how to add photos, answer
  reviews politely, and post an update — plus "never pay anyone who
  cold-calls about your Google listing."
- [ ] Log time spent → after 3 builds, set the real per-client number in
  the catalog (A5).
