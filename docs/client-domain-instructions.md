# Your Domain, In Your Name — 10-Minute Setup

> Client-facing doc, referenced by the handoff runbook ("send them the
> 5-step instructions"). Send at kickoff to any client who doesn't
> already own a domain. Written for someone who has never bought one.

Owning your web address yourself is the whole point of this build — it
means nobody (including us) can ever hold your website hostage. Here's
the ten-minute version.

## 1. Go to a registrar

Use **Cloudflare Registrar** (dash.cloudflare.com — at-cost pricing,
~$10/year) or **Porkbun** (porkbun.com — nearly as cheap, slightly
friendlier). Avoid the heavily-advertised ones that charge $9.99 the
first year and $45 after.

## 2. Search for your name

Try `yourbusinessname.com` first. Taken? Add your city
(`smithplumbingfresno.com`) before reaching for odd endings — customers
still trust `.com` most.

## 3. Buy it — with these two settings

- **Auto-renew: ON.** Expired domains get bought by squatters the same
  day. This is the one setting that matters.
- **WHOIS privacy: ON** (usually free and automatic now). Keeps your
  home address out of the public record.

## 4. Lock down the account

Use a password you don't use anywhere else, and turn on two-factor
authentication (the code-from-your-phone thing). This account now holds
your business's address — treat it like the bank.

## 5. Tell us it's done

Send us the domain name. When it's time to launch, we'll send you the
two or three DNS records to paste in (with a screen-share if you'd like)
— or you can invite us as a limited DNS collaborator. **Never send
anyone your registrar password, including us.**

That's it. Total cost: about $10–20/year, billed by the registrar to
your card, forever yours.
