# Handoff email + referral ask — drafts

> Sent at launch of every free build. Merge fields in [brackets]. Voice:
> plain, warm, zero pressure — the referral ask rides on a moment of
> goodwill, so it stays one sentence.

---

## The handoff email (walk-away client)

**Subject:** [Business name] is live — and it's all yours

Hi [First name],

Your site is live at [domain]. Everything is in your name: the domain,
the hosting, the code, the form that sends messages to your inbox. The
attached one-pager lists every login and where it lives.

Also attached:
- A short recorded walkthrough of how to make simple edits
- Your "how to get changes made" note — email me anytime; small fixes I
  caused are free for the next 60 days, and anything new gets a plain
  written quote first

No subscription started, nothing auto-bills. If you ever want the
hosting-and-updates plan later ($150/month, cancel anytime), it's there;
if not, this is goodbye and the site is still yours.

One favor, only if you're happy: if you know one other business owner
who needs what you just got, forward them [site link]. That's how this
works.

— [Name], Signalworks

## The handoff email (Care Plan client)

Same as above, with the middle paragraph swapped:

> Your Care Plan is active: I handle hosting, updates, backups, and
> support, and content edits land within 2 business days — just email
> what changed. Cancel anytime; if you do, everything moves into your
> name within 5 business days at no charge.

## The referral line (for invoices, follow-ups, GBP replies)

> Know another business that needs a site? The free build works the same
> for them: [site link].

## Footer credit option (pending your decision)

If you approve the footer-credit idea, client sites get one quiet line:

> Site by Signalworks — built free. [link]

The signed agreement then needs one added sentence: "The site footer may
include a one-line credit with a link to Signalworks; you may opt out at
any time and we'll remove it within 5 business days." Opt-out keeps it
honest and un-pushy.
