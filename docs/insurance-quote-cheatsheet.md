# Insurance quote cheat sheet: Free Website Co.

Goal: general liability (GL) and tech E&O quotes from Hiscox, NEXT, Thimble and Insureon in 30 minutes. Paste these answers into each form.

## 1. How to classify the business

- **NAICS 541511, Custom Computer Programming Services.** Its official index includes "Web (i.e., Internet) page design services, custom" ([NAICS.com](https://www.naics.com/naics-code-description/?code=541511)). Matching SIC: **7371, Computer Programming Services** ([SICCODE.com](https://siccode.com/naics-code/541511/custom-computer-programming-services)).
- **Class name per insurer:** Hiscox "web designer" ([Hiscox](https://www.hiscox.com/small-business-insurance/professional-business-insurance/web-design-insurance)); NEXT "Web Designer" ([NEXT](https://www.nextinsurance.com/business/web-designer-insurance/)); Thimble "Graphic and Web Designers" or "Web Developers," which routes to its Software Developer product ([Thimble](https://www.thimble.com/professional-liability-insurance/coverage)); Insureon "Web and UX / UI designers" ([Insureon](https://www.insureon.com/technology-business-insurance/web-designers)).

Sources for the fields: Hiscox's application asks "Projected annual (12 months) gross revenue" in bands starting at $0 to $50,000, and "Is E&O coverage currently in force?" ([Hiscox form](https://www.hiscox.com/documents/brokers/misc-pl/10783_Entrepreneurs_Professional_Liability_Consultants_Application.pdf)). NEXT asks profession, address, structure, start year, "estimated annual payroll and revenue," then yes/no claims-history questions; about 10 minutes ([NEXT](https://www.nextinsurance.com/blog/business-insurance-quote-online/)). Thimble asks ZIP, crew size, coverage length and coverage limit ($1M or $2M; Thimble says most businesses pick $2M because the price difference is small) ([Thimble](https://www.thimble.com/industry/professional-services-business-insurance/software-developer)). Insureon asks current and projected revenue, largest-client revenue, percent of clients with written contracts, and whether subcontractors carry E&O ([Insureon](https://www.insureon.com/small-business-insurance/professional-liability/application)).

## 3. Limits to buy

- **GL $1M per occurrence / $2M aggregate.** What client contracts usually require, and Insureon's standard web-designer policy.
- **E&O $1M.** The most common solo choice (84% of Thimble freelancers) and Insureon's average tech E&O policy ($1M/$1M, $2,500 deductible).

## 4. What it should cost (insurers' own published figures)

| Insurer | GL | E&O |
|---|---|---|
| Hiscox | $29.17/mo for a CA IT consultant, $500K/$2M ([source](https://www.hiscox.com/small-business-insurance/general-liability-insurance/general-liability-insurance-cost)) | "As little as $23 per month"; $42.92/mo for a CA IT consultant, $150K revenue, $500K limit ([source](https://www.hiscox.com/small-business-insurance/professional-liability-insurance/cost)) |
| NEXT | "As low as $19/month"; 45% pay $45 or less | "As low as $19/month"; 73% pay $45 or less ([source](https://www.nextinsurance.com/business-insurance/cost/)) |
| Thimble | Median $7.20/mo, freelancers | GL+PL bundle median $60/mo; 2022 data ([source](https://www.thimble.com/blog/freelancer-consultant-insurance-cost)) |
| Insureon | $38/mo, $1M/$2M | Tech E&O $94/mo, $1M/$1M; updated June 22, 2026 ([source](https://www.insureon.com/technology-business-insurance/web-designers/cost)) |

Expect roughly **$40 to $140 a month for both — this is my estimate from the table above, not a figure any insurer publishes.** The low end is NEXT's published floors ($19 + $19); Hiscox's worked examples land near $72; Insureon's medians land near $132. Cyber is separate; Insureon puts it at $111/mo.

## 5. Five questions to ask before buying

1. Does E&O cover work product built with AI tools? Is there any AI or automated-content exclusion?
2. If a client site is hacked or a contact form leaks customer data, does this policy respond, or do I need cyber?
3. The 60-day bug-fix promise and five-day draft promise are contractual. Is breach of contract or late delivery excluded? Is the cost of redoing the work covered?
4. This is claims-made. What is my retroactive date, and what happens if I cancel or switch carriers?
5. Is copyright infringement (stock photos, fonts, AI images) covered, and at what limit? Ask specifically: Hiscox publishes $200,000 of **software** copyright coverage for "most IT consultants and technology service providers" — that is a narrower thing than image or font infringement, and it is not stated for the web-designer class. Since your clients supply photos and logos, this is the question to press hardest. Note $200,000 would itself fall below the $250K sublimit floor flagged as a red flag below.

## 6. Red flags

- A GL-only policy sold as "web design insurance." GL excludes professional mistakes; you need both.
- Hourly or by-the-job E&O. Claims arrive months later; E&O must stay continuously in force.
- Defense costs inside the limit, or IP sublimits under $250K.
- Being steered into "IT consultant" or "software publisher" when a web-design class exists.
- Guessing revenue low to cut premium. Hiscox's form warns false or misleading answers can void the policy.
- Thimble's E&O is not sold in NY or WA. Fine for CA; matters if you move.

## Fact-check corrections applied 2026-09-06

An independent pass re-opened every cited source. 27 of 30 claims held. Three were corrected above: Thimble's fourth rating input (coverage limit), the combined premium range (now labeled as an estimate, floor lowered to $40 to match NEXT's own published figures), and the Hiscox copyright figure, which covers *software* copyright for IT consultants and is not the stock-photo or AI-image coverage the first draft implied.

## Caveats and unverified points

- The $25,000 projected first-year revenue is my placeholder, chosen to sit inside Hiscox's lowest band ($0-$50,000) for a zero-client launch; owner must confirm. Understating revenue can void a policy, so pick an honest number.
- Hiscox publishes no web-designer-specific price. Its $29.17/mo GL and $42.92/mo PL examples are for an 'IT consultant' in California; the $23/mo figure is its general 'as little as' PL price. A '$22.50/mo' web-design figure appeared only in a search snippet and could not be verified on the Hiscox page, so it was not used.
- The Hiscox application cited is the broker-channel 'Entrepreneurs Professional Liability - Consultants' form (dated 09/12). It was used as a proxy for the online quote questions; the live online flow may word fields differently.
- Thimble's dollar figures come from a February 2022 blog post and are medians across all freelancer types, not web designers specifically. Thimble's product pages give no dollar prices, only the rating factors (ZIP, crew size, coverage length, $1M or $2M limit).
- NEXT publishes no web-design-specific prices; the $19/mo and percentage figures are company-wide. The full list of NEXT's yes/no history questions (felony, bankruptcy, largest contract, office vs. offsite percentage) came from search-result summaries and was not confirmed on a NEXT page I read.
- Insureon's figures are medians for its own applicants; its web-designer and web-developer cost pages show identical numbers (updated June 22, 2026).
- I did not run any live quote form (they require entering personal data and submitting), so exact field labels in each insurer's live flow are unverified. Web pages from Hiscox, Thimble and census.gov blocked plain fetches; they were read through a JavaScript-rendering fetcher.
- The 'no hosting of client data' answer follows the task brief. If the $29/mo Hosted plan means the owner administers hosting accounts on clients' behalf, that should be described accurately to the insurer.
- The $50-$140/mo combined range is my synthesis of the cited figures, not a published number. NAICS 541430 (Graphic Design Services) is an alternative only if a form forces a visual-only class.
