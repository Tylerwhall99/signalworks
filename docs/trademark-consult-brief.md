## Who we are / intended use

Signalworks: web design services — free websites for home services
businesses, paid monthly services (lead capture, reputation/review
management, hosting/site care), and quoted custom builds. Operating
under holding company Long Hall Financial (entity structure TBD with
CPA). Intended classes of use: **042** (website design/development,
SaaS-adjacent services), likely **035** (advertising/marketing services)
as services expand.
