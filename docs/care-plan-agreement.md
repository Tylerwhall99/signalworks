## What every plan covers

1. **Hosting** of your website on infrastructure we manage, with
   updates, backups, and monitoring — downtime noticed by us before you.
2. **Edit quantities as listed for your plan.** An edit is one request
   email covering up to three small changes to existing pages (hours,
   prices, photos, wording); new pages and redesign-scale changes are
   add-ons, quoted in writing first. Unused edits don't roll over.
3. **Support** from the person who built the site, at your plan's reply
   time.
4. **Bug coverage** applies to defects in what we built. If a
   third-party service changes underneath the site (form provider,
   embed, browser), diagnosis is free and any rebuild is quoted in
   writing first.
5. **If we're unavailable** (illness, emergency), reply clocks pause up
   to 10 business days; beyond that you may cancel with the standard
   full handoff. *(Attorney: bless or tighten.)*

## What stays true the whole time

6. **You still own everything.** The domain stays in your registrar
   account. The site's code remains yours.
7. **Cancel anytime, no fee, no notice period.** On cancellation we
   complete the full handoff within **5 business days at no charge** —
   code, hosting, and every credential moved into accounts in your name,
   confirmed in writing.
8. **No lock-in by design.** Bug fixes for things we built stay covered
   per your build agreement; leaving the Care Plan never voids them.
9. **Billing stops the day you cancel;** any part-month already billed
   is refunded pro-rata.
