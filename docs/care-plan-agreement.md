## What the Care Plan covers

1. **Hosting** of your website on infrastructure we manage.
2. **Updates, backups, and monitoring** — software kept current, the
   site backed up, downtime noticed by us before you.
3. **Content edits within 2 business days** — email what changed

   edits to existing pages are included; new pages or redesign-scale
   changes are quoted in writing first.
4. **Support** from the person who built the site, by email or call,
   replies within one business day.

## What stays true the whole time

5. **You still own everything.** The domain stays in your registrar
   account. The site's code remains yours.
6. **Cancel anytime, no fee, no notice period.** On cancellation we
   complete the full handoff within **5 business days at no charge** —
   code, hosting, and every credential moved into accounts in your name,
   confirmed in writing.
7. **No lock-in by design.** Bug fixes for things we built stay covered
   per your build agreement; leaving the Care Plan never voids them.
8. **Billing stops the day you cancel;** any part-month already billed
   is refunded pro-rata.
