# Google Business Profile setup sheet: The Free Website Co.

Prepared 2026-09-06. Paste-ready. Anything in [brackets] needs your input. Character counts were checked against Google's current limits.

## Rules that changed recently (checked today)

- **Q&A is gone.** Google shut down the Business Profile Q&A API on Nov 3, 2025 and began removing the public Q&A section on Dec 3, 2025. It is replaced by "Ask Maps," an AI answer box that pulls from your profile, your website, and reviews. The five Q&A pairs below go on your website FAQ and into posts instead (section 11).
- **Description cap is 750 characters.** No links, no HTML, and Google says not to focus on prices or promotions.
- **Service area: max 20 areas, cities/postal codes/counties only.** No states, no countries. Whole area should sit within about two hours' drive of where you are based.
- **Home-based service business: do not enter your address.** Google's own wording: if you run the business from a residential address, clear the address from the profile.
- **Posts cap at 1,500 characters.** All-caps and heavy punctuation can get a post removed.

## 13. What NOT to do

- No fake reviews. No paid, discounted, or gifted reviews. No reviews from friends, family, or your own account. Google bans incentives and "review gating" (asking only happy customers). Ask everyone, offer nothing.
- No keyword-stuffed name. "The Free Website Co." and nothing else.
- No home address, virtual office, or P.O. box on the profile.
- No more than 20 service areas. No states or countries.
- No links, prices, or promo phrasing in the description.
- No attributes that are not true, especially identity attributes and "online appointments."
- No call-tracking or forwarding number as the main phone. Google requires a number that connects to the business.
- No second profile for "nationwide" or for another city. One business, one profile.
- No all-caps, exclamation marks in a row, or emoji in posts.
- No testimonials, client counts, "years in business," logos, or certifications anywhere on the profile. There are none yet.
- No stock "team" photos.
- Do not ignore the profile after setup. Reply to every review within a day or two, including the bad ones, in plain language.
